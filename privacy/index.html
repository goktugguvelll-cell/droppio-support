Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Droppio is an independently developed and published game by Goktug Guvel. This policy describes information handled by the current game.

Information We Store

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences in your browser on your device. This may include saved games, scores, level and daily-star progress, themes, backgrounds, difficulty choices, and sound or vibration settings. The current game does not ask you to create an account or provide a username, email address, or other personal details.

How Information Is Used

Device-stored information is used to save your progress and remember your preferences when you return to the game. The current game does not submit scores to an online leaderboard.

Third-Party Services

The project retains Firebase Hosting configuration for publishing the website. The current game does not use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, or Cloud Functions for accounts or leaderboards. Removing this code does not delete data that may have been stored by an earlier version; we have not verified whether any such records remain. No advertising, analytics, or tracking integration was found in the current game client. Game fonts and media are served with the game.

Children’s Privacy

The current game does not request personal details or require a player account. Game progress and preferences are stored on the device. Parents or guardians with questions about the game or possible legacy data can contact us using the details below.

Data Storage and Deletion

Game progress and preferences remain in your browser’s local storage until you clear the Droppio site data using your browser settings or the browser removes it. Clearing this data removes the information stored on that device and may reset your progress and preferences. It does not delete any legacy online records that may have been stored by an earlier version. We have not verified whether such records remain. Contact us with questions about legacy data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game or its information practices change. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact Droppio Support at support@droppio.pro.
