Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Choose a game mode from the main menu and follow the on-screen hints. Each mode is designed to be quick to learn and relaxing to play.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The games run on your device, so you can play without an internet connection.

How do I turn sound on or off?

Use the sound control in the game's settings. Also make sure your iPhone is not in Silent mode and the media volume is turned up.

I hear no sound after switching apps.

Tap the screen once after returning to the game to re-enable audio. If the sound is still missing, fully close and reopen Droppio.

The game is slow or not responding.

Close other apps, restart Droppio, and make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I report a bug?

Email us using the address above and describe what happened and what you were doing when it occurred. Screenshots are welcome.
